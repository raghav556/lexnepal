# Convex-to-Next.js Migration Risk Register

**Status:** Initial baseline  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-02  
**Review cadence:** Weekly during migration and before every phase exit

## Scoring

- Probability: 1 (rare) to 5 (almost certain).
- Impact: 1 (minor) to 5 (critical).
- Exposure: probability × impact.
- Priority: Critical 20–25, High 12–19, Medium 6–11, Low 1–5.

Only the named risk owner may accept or close a risk. Until Phase 0 owners are nominated, ownership is assigned to accountable roles.
